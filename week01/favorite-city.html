My Favorite City

From my travel experience, i have been to different cities, But one of My favorite city is Cotonou in Benin Republic, a country in west africa. The city is known for it's high level of serenity (always clean and in order) and intense hospitality of the people there. They're entirely loyal, simple, cheerful and respectful, always bowing and almost portraying flat on the floor while greeting tourists they have never met before irrespective of their age. As an african from west africa i have never seen people that have respect for culture and tradition like the Beninese. Beninese are one of the best and kindest people i have met here on earth. That's one of the trip i would not forget in a hurry.
